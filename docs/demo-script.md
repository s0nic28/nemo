# NEMO v0.1 Demo Script

1. Open the live app and let the startup animation play.
2. Move the cursor around the screen and show NEMO's gaze following the environment.
3. Click NEMO to trigger a local reaction.
4. Send a short chat message and show thinking → response → speech.
5. Stop interacting and show the idle scheduler continue changing NEMO's mood/behavior.
6. Briefly open the repository and explain that AI reasoning is separated from local animation.

Do not demonstrate camera, memory, games, or vision as completed until they are actually implemented.
